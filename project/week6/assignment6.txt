Week 6: Profile Card
Box Model, Display & Positioning

QUESTION
Make a profile card with a round photo circle and a scrolling bio box. Add a top bar that stays on screen and a round + button that floats at the bottom.

6.1 Use box-sizing: border-box. Play with padding, border and margin. Take a DevTools screenshot and label the box model
6.2 Make the card with a border, round corners and a circle avatar. Then copy it to make 2 colour versions
6.3 Give the bio box a fixed height and overflow: auto so it scrolls
6.4 Make the top bar sticky and the + button fixed. Use z-index
6.5 Clean up your class names, test it, and submit
